## The 5 Red Flags

☐ "Act right now."

☐ "Don't tell anyone."

☐ Pay with gift cards / crypto / wire.

☐ "Give me your password."

---

## Ask Yourself

- Was I expecting this?
- Do I know this person?
- Have I verified independently?
- Am I being pressured?

---

## Banks Will NOT:

- Ask for your password
- Move money to a "safe account"
- Ask you to buy gift cards

---

## Government Will NOT:

- Demand immediate payment by phone
- Accept gift cards
- Threaten arrest on a surprise call

---

## If It Sounds Like Family

Hang up.

Call the number you already have.

Never send money first.

---

## If You Already Shared Info

1. Call your bank.
2. Change passwords.
3. Tell a trusted person.
4. Report it.

*Act fast — options shrink with time.*

---

## The Golden Rules

Slow down · Verify · Never trust caller ID

Never pay with gift cards · Never share passwords

Hang up when in doubt

---

## One Thing Never Changes

Technology changes. Tactics change.

Scammers want you to **react before you think.**
