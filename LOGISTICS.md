# Presentation Logistics

Equipment and room setup needed to deliver this course.

## Required Equipment

- **Projector or large screen** with HDMI (or wireless casting) connection, for displaying [deck.md](deck.md).
- **Microphone** (handheld or lapel), especially for rooms larger than a small classroom, since the audience is seniors who may have hearing difficulty.
- **Speakers / sound system** if the room isn't already wired for audio.
- **Extension cord / power strip** for laptop and projector.

## Seating & Accessibility

- Chairs arranged so all attendees can see the screen without obstruction.
- Reserve accessible seating near the front for attendees with mobility or hearing needs.
- **A chair on stage/at the presenting area for the presenter.**

## Room Setup Checklist

- [ ] Projector/screen tested with the deck before the session starts
- [ ] Microphone tested and audible from the back of the room
- [ ] Presenter chair placed within reach of the presenting area
- [ ] Power outlets accessible for laptop/projector
